Addis Ababa , Bole atlas , Namibia Avenue
Azzeman Hotel is a four star hotel located at fast growing district in Addis Ababa just 2 km from bole international airport next to 2000 Habesha cultural restaurant. In addition to its proximity to the airport, the hotel is surrounded by shops, cafes, Burger house, restaurant, bar and clubs. The hotel offers different types of spacious accommodations. All the rooms &suits features flat screen TV, Coffee and tea maker, Wi-Fi, mini bar, write table, Air-conditioning, shower, satellite channels, safe deposit box, wardrobe, hair dryer, Electronic kettle, coffee table with chairs.
