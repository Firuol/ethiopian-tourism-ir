Title: Dessie

The capital of the South Wollo Zone in North Shewa, Dessie has a mix of cultural and historical sites, including Ayeteyefe Hall and the local museum. The town is located 400 km (250 miles) from Addis Ababa, and is easily accessible by road or direct flight, providing an opportunity to explore the city's scenic surroundings.
Getting there
Ethiopian Airlines offers direct flights between Addis Ababa Bole International Airport and Dessie Kombolcha Airport. You can book tickets directly through the Ethiopian Airlines website, mobile app or through travel agencies. While road travel might be more scenic, it can be time-consuming due to road conditions and traffic. Public buses and private taxis are available for this route.
For history and culture enthusiasts, the Dessie Museum is a must-visit. This museum houses a collection of artefacts that showcase the town’s rich history and culture, providing valuable insights into its past. Exploring the city's diverse religious landscape by visiting local churches and mosques offers a deeper understanding of the local culture and traditions.
For those seeking natural beauty, Dessie provides ample opportunities. The city is surrounded by scenic mountains, offering excellent hiking and trekking trails. A short drive from Dessie, the Simien Mountains National Park, a UNESCO World Heritage Site, boasts breathtaking views of mountains, valleys, and diverse wildlife.
Getting around
Taxis are readily available in Dessie and are a convenient way to get around the city. Tuk-tuks are another popular mode of transport in Dessie, especially for shorter distances. Dessie is a relatively walkable city, and exploring the city on foot can be a great way to experience its charm and interact with the locals.
