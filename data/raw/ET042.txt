Title: Mekele

Source: Visit Ethiopia
Topic: destination
License: Ministry of Tourism of Ethiopia public webpage; URL retained

Mekele is the capital city of Tigray and serves as a gateway to historical sites in the region. It is close to the Adi-Abun and Abreha-We-Atsbeha rock-hewn churches. Visitors can explore nearby rock-hewn churches, ancient sites, and a museum featuring cultural and historical artifacts.
Getting There
Direct flights to Mekele are available through Ethiopian Airlines. For a road trip, Mekele is about 780 km (485 miles) from Addis Ababa, passing through the picturesque Tigray highlands.
Getting Around
Most visitors explore Mekelle with guided tours. Local taxis or walking are convenient options.
