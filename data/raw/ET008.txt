Title: Bahir Dar
URL: https://visitethiopia.et/space/bahir-dar
Source: Visit Ethiopia
Topic: destination
License: Ministry of Tourism of Ethiopia public webpage; URL retained

This lakeside town is a popular tourism hub. It offers a blend of cultural and natural attractions. Key highlights include Lake Tana and the Blue Nile Falls. Visitors can also explore the Palace and the Royal Archive. Strolling along the Blue Nile River is another great option. The town has a relaxed charm. It’s an ideal spot for unwinding by the water.
Getting There
Ethiopian Airlines offers direct flights from Addis Ababa to Bahir Dar. Alternatively, the scenic drive from Addis Ababa to Bahir Dar is about 550 km (340 miles), passing through lush valleys and breathtaking landscapes.
Getting Around
Most visitors explore Bahir Dar with guided tours. Local taxis or walking are convenient options.
