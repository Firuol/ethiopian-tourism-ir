Menelik II Avenue, Kirkos, 1164 Addis Ababa, Ethiopia
Hilton Addis Ababa is the pioneer of International Hotels in Ethiopia and was opened in November 3, 1969 by the late Emperor Haile Selassie. Hilton Addis Ababa is set within lush gardens, our urban retreat is near ECA Convention Center, Unity Park, Science & Technology Museum, and Friendship Park and a 15 minutes ride from Addis Ababa Bole International Airport.
The 12 story majestic building of the Hilton Addis Ababa is designed in the form of the Lalibela churches, which comes from one of the most popular tourist attractions in Ethiopia that is considered the 8th wonder of the world, with an interior decor of typical motifs of Ethiopian arts. The Hotel is sited on 15 acres of land and has 372 Guest Rooms and Suites
The hotel has 2 Restaurants (Gazebo Restaurant on the Park has a Specialty Italian Offering by the side and its Sunday Brunch with live entertainment is popular among other offers like Jazz Night and BBQ. The hotel has also 2 Bars, a Pastry Shop and Café. The hotel has a 24 hours open Room Service, six function rooms, an executive floor which is an exclusive hotel within a hotel with a beautiful Lounge on top of the building. Among the sport and relaxation services offered are the only thermally heated cross-shaped swimming pool in Addis Ababa, four tennis courts, ladies and gent’s sauna, massage, squash court, mini-golf and other recreation facilities are found. A business center for prompts communication and courier service and a modern and efficient Laundry and Valet services.
For the sake of convenience; the Ethiopian Airlines office, Express Travel, Kenya Airways, 3 banks (one operating 24/7), Duty Free Shop, Jewelry shop, Art gallery, Flower Shop, Supermarket and beauty salon are found in the hotel.
