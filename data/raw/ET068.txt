At the Best Western Premier Dynasty Hotel, we believe that you should never have to compromise when it comes to comfort. That's why we've ensured that every room is spacious, fully carpeted, and air-conditioned. Our spacious rooms are perfect for business or leisure guests who want a little extra space to spread out and relax. In addition to our great rooms, you'll also find a meeting facility for all kinds of meetings, a swimming pool, luxurious gym, a steam and sauna, and all of this is located at Bole area in the centre of Addis Ababa.
