Forbes Africa Special Report – Ethiopia 2025
Ethiopia is a country with a rich, diverse cultural heritage shaped by over 80 ethnic groups and languages such as Amharic, Oromo, Tigrinya, and Somali. Its cuisine is famous for injera (spongy flatbread) served with spicy stews like doro wat and kitfo, and enjoyed in a communal style.
