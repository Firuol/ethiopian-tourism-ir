Title: 3-Day Tigray Churches &amp; Gheralta Trekking Tour

License: Ministry of Tourism of Ethiopia public webpage; URL retained

Pickup: Addis Ababa Bole Internatonal airport
Discover the breathtaking rock-hewn churches of Tigray — Ethiopia’s most dramatic and remote spiritual wonders carved high into the cliffs of the Gheralta Mountains. This 3-day tour from Mekelle takes you deep into the northern highlands to explore ancient churches, rugged landscapes, and traditional villages.
You’ll hike to some of the most iconic and spiritually significant churches, including Maryam Korkor, Daniel Korkor, and the awe-inspiring Abuna Yemata Guh, carved into a vertical cliff with panoramic views. Stay two nights in a comfortable local guesthouse or eco-lodge in Hawzen, and enjoy warm Tigrayan hospitality, local cuisine, and expert guiding.
Tour Highlights:
Explore legendary rock-hewn churches such as Abuna Yemata Guh, Maryam Korkor, and Daniel Korkor
Trek through the dramatic Gheralta Mountains
Learn the history of ancient monastic life and Tigrayan Orthodox traditions
Experience local life in Hawzen and nearby villages
Enjoy panoramic views, cultural interactions, and peaceful highland scenery
