Savor the flavors of Addis Ababa at Radisson Blu
Embark on a culinary journey like no other at Radisson Blu Hotel, Addis Ababa. From our expansive Super Breakfast Buffet to deliciously prepared French brasserie-style dishes, contemporary soul food, and handcrafted signature cocktails, every meal is a celebration of flavor. Don't miss the chance to indulge in Ethiopia's world-renowned coffee, prepared to perfection.
Add a touch of magic to your week with our vibrant Arabian Night every Tuesday, featuring tantalizing dishes, a captivating ambiance, and an experience that will transport you straight to the heart of the Middle East.
Whether it's an intimate dinner or a lively gathering, let our memorable service and unforgettable atmosphere make every bite a moment to remember.
Discover our professional event spaces in Addis Ababa
Elevate your meeting or conference with our 10 elegant venues at Radisson Blu Hotel, Addis Ababa. Whether you're planning an intimate gathering of 12 or hosting a grand event for up to 350 delegates, our versatile spaces offer the perfect setting for a successful and memorable occasion.
Take advantage of our comprehensive business center, tailored catering options, and personalized service to ensure a seamless event. Our dedicated team will work closely with you to meet your specific needs, from room setup to audiovisual requirements. Beyond business, our event spaces are also ideal for social functions, including weddings, receptions, and galas.
Escape to an oasis of tranquility and wellness at Rainforest Day Spa
Indulge in complete relaxation and serenity at Radisson Blu Hotel, Addis Ababa. Discover an inviting and tranquil experience that will melt away tensions and reawaken your senses. Treat yourself to rejuvenating wellness therapies, from refreshing facials and soothing massages to manicures and pedicures. Let our expert stylists enhance your natural beauty with some new highlights or a haircut.
For a revitalizing experience, keep up with your fitness goals and enjoy energizing workouts in our state-of-the-art fitness center. From our spa treatments to our gym facilities, experience a holistic approach to well-being at the Rainforest Day Spa.
