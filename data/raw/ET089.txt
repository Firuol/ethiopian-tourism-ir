Pickup: Semera airport
Embark on an unforgettable 3-day expedition into the Danakil Depression starting from Semera, the gateway to one of the most geologically active and visually striking places on Earth. Experience Erta Ale, an active volcano with a bubbling lava lake, walk across endless salt flats, and explore the kaleidoscopic terrain of Dallol, where sulfur springs and acidic pools paint the desert in surreal colors.
This tour also gives you a glimpse into the lives of the Afar people, known for their resilience and centuries-old salt mining traditions. Travel in reliable 4x4 vehicles with experienced local guides, and camp under the stars in one of the harshest but most fascinating environments on the planet.
Tour Highlights:
Trek to the glowing Erta Ale Volcano
Explore the vibrant hot springs of Dallol
Walk on the otherworldly salt flats of Lake Asale
Witness camel caravans and traditional salt mining
Experience Afar hospitality and culture
Camp in the desert beneath the stars
