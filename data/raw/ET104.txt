Forbes Africa Special Report – Ethiopia 2025
Ethiopia offers diverse outdoor adventures beyond hiking and trekking in the Simien and Bale Mountains. Visitors can climb Erta Ale volcano, explore the Danakil Depression, go ballooning over the Rift Valley, or cave in the Sof Omar Caves. The country also hosts high-altitude running events and offers opportunities for rock climbing, cycling, and white-water rafting on rivers like the Blue Nile. Ethiopia’s landscapes and wildlife make it a paradise for photographers and adventure seekers alike.
