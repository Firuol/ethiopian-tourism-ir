Pickup: Addis Ababa Bole Internatonal airport
Dive into the vibrant flavors of Ethiopia on this half-day Addis Ababa Food Tour, a delicious journey through traditional dishes, local markets, and coffee culture. Perfect for food lovers and cultural explorers, this guided experience takes you deep into the heart of Ethiopian cuisine.
The tour begins at a local market, where you’ll explore stalls bursting with fresh produce, spices, and traditional ingredients. Learn about injera, berbere, and how Ethiopia’s rich culinary culture is rooted in community and history.
Continue to authentic restaurants and food stops to sample signature dishes such as doro wot (chicken stew), tibs, kitfo, and various vegetarian delights. End the tour with a traditional Ethiopian coffee ceremony or a stop at Tomoca Coffee, one of the oldest roasters in the city.
Tour Highlights:
Guided walk through a bustling local market
Taste iconic Ethiopian dishes at hand-picked restaurants
Learn about food preparation, spices, and traditions
