Opened in 2010, Soramba Hotel represents and brings it to the forefront of four star accommodation venues in this rapidly growing East Africa hub. Just 10 minutes drive away from Bole International Air-port, the hotel is conveniently located at the exact center of the city where a number of amenities lay around it.
Banks, supermarkets, embassies, The African Union, ECA, Addis Ababa City Hall and Federal Police. the National Palace, colleges, ancient tourist attractions, and animal parks are some of the places that are within the stretch of the hotel. Merkato, the largest open-air market in Africa, is also a minute drive away from the hotel. On arrival at the Hotel you will be warmly welcomed by our staff and find yourself in our spacious lobby where a private registration desk awaits you.
