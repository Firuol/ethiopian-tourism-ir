Title: Local SIM Cards
URL: https://visitethiopia.et/news/ministry-to-develop-5-new-tourist-destinations
Source: Visit Ethiopia
Topic: news
License: Ministry of Tourism of Ethiopia public webpage; URL retained

Travel tip
Tourists have the option to choose between SIM cards from Ethio Telecom and Safaricom, with Ethio Telecom boasting nationwide network coverage. Mobile phone usage has expanded significantly in recent decades, with services now accessible throughout the country. Internet services have also seen major advancements, including the widespread availability of 4G networks and the introduction of 5G in Addis Ababa and other cities and towns.
