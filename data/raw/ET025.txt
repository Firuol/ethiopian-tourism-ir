Title: Gedeo Cultural Landscape
URL: https://visitethiopia.et/space/gedeo-cultural-landscape
Source: Visit Ethiopia
Topic: destination
License: Ministry of Tourism of Ethiopia public webpage; URL retained

The Gedeo Cultural Landscape is a remarkable blend of natural and cultural heritage. It features an intricate agroforestry system, stunning megalithic monuments, mesmerizing rock art sites, and sacred ritual forests. These incredible achievements, crafted by the Gedeo people over generations, have been recognized as a UNESCO World Heritage Site.
At the core of this unique landscape lies an agroforestry system rooted in centuries of indigenous knowledge. The Gedeo people cultivate diverse food crops, led by enset and coffee trees, while preserving the surrounding natural forest as a haven for biodiversity and environmental sustainability. This harmonious coexistence of culture and nature underscores the Gedeo Cultural Landscape’s distinction as a World Heritage Site.
